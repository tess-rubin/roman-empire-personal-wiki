# Source distribution and attribution

This repository is an educational assignment. It does not grant blanket rights to third-party material.

## Tacitus

Tacitus, *Annals* XV, translated by Alfred John Church and William Jackson Brodribb. The Internet Classics Archive credits its HTML presentation to Daniel C. Stevenson / Web Atomics (1994–2009). Its [permissions page](https://web.mit.edu/classics/Help/permissions.html), checked September 29, 2026, permits educational distribution of works but separately restricts copying pages.

**The MIT HTML presentation is excluded from this repository.** Run `python3 scripts/download_sources.py` while connected to obtain the unchanged original directly from the archive and verify its recorded SHA-256. It remains local and ignored by Git. No claim of permission to redistribute that HTML is made. Source links in the notes and saved evidence resolve after this setup step. The evidence records contain historical translation passages, not the archive's page layout. No historical text, extraction, source identity or evaluated model output was changed to accomplish publication.

## Augustus

Augustus, *Res Gestae*, translated by Frederick W. Shipley (1924), from LacusCurtius. The downloaded page identifies its public-domain status. Its attribution and notices remain intact.

## Pliny and Trajan

*Letters of Pliny*, William Melmoth translation, Project Gutenberg ebook 2811. Full original attribution and Gutenberg terms remain in the included HTML. Follow those terms and applicable local law when reusing it.

## Recorded evidence

Publication copies redact the local home-directory path as `<LOCAL_HOME>`. Original unredacted records are retained only in ignored `results/private/`. Model responses, source quotations, timings and outcomes are preserved. Runtime server logs are excluded; command transcripts, network samples and model-call records remain included.
